# Area Leader Guide
<!-- Rewritten by Claude AI on 2026-09-28 -->

Thank you for agreeing to lead the team in your count area.

The registration system at birdcount.ca will help you keep track of who is registered to participate in your count area. You will automatically receive emails (described below), or you can access your team information online at `https://<circle>.cbc.birdcount.ca/leader`, e.g. https://vancouver.cbc.birdcount.ca/leader. (The first time you visit this URL, you will be prompted to enter your email address. The website will then email you a link you can use to sign in to your leader page.)

## Emails you'll receive

The system sends you automatic updates about your team, without you having to check the dashboard:

- **Team update**, sent when someone joins or leaves your team.
- **Weekly summary**, a standing reminder of your full current roster.

Both include a **View Leader Dashboard** button and a full contact list at the time the email went out.
Exactly which days and times these go out is set by your count's organizer. If you're not getting them, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).


## Signing in

There are no passwords. You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email.

1. Go to your circle's address and add `/leader` to it: `https://<circle>.birdcount.ca/leader`,
   for example `https://vancouver.cbc.birdcount.ca/leader`. If you aren't signed in, you'll be taken to a login page
   titled "Admin / Leader Login".
2. Type your email address — the one your organizer registered you under — and click **Send login
   link**.
3. Open the email and click the link. It stops working after **15 minutes** and can only be used once.
4. On the page that opens, click **Continue signing in**.

A few things to know:

- Use the exact email address your organizer has on file for you. If you use a different one, no email
  will arrive.
- If the link email doesn't show up, check spam or junk first, then try again.
- Some workplace email systems open every link in an email to scan it for viruses, which can burn
  through the link before you click it yourself. If a link says it's already expired the moment you
  open it, request a fresh one and click it right away. If it keeps happening, tell your organizer.  You may need to sign up with a different email address.
- The buttons in your team-update and weekly-summary emails ("View Leader Dashboard") take you straight
  to `/leader` and start the same sign-in process.
- Click **Logout** (top right) when you're done if you're using a shared or public computer.  The login will otherwise expire after one week.

## Your dashboard

Signing in takes you straight to your dashboard for your assigned area. It shows:

- Your own name, email and phone number(s) as recorded in the system, and which area you're leading.
- A **team summary**. For Christmas Bird Counts, there will be an indication of regular (field) counters versus
  **FEEDER** counters (people counting birds from home rather than in the field).
- Your full team, in tables (see [Your team](#your-team) below).
- An **Export Team CSV** button (see [below](#exporting-your-team-as-a-csv)).
- Year tabs across the top if you have more than one year of data — see [Counters from past years](#counters-from-past-years).

**Leading more than one area.** A few circles have one person leading two areas at once. This needs a
separate registration for each area — ask your organizer to set it up, since the site needs one record
per area even though both use the same email address. If you lead more than one area, you'll see a row
of area tabs above the dashboard; each one shows only that area's team, name and contact details. The
**Export Team CSV** button always exports just the area you're currently viewing, and each area's
team-update and weekly-summary emails link straight to that area's own tab.

## "Your team" on the dashboard

The list of people on your team is split into two tables, **Regular Participants** and **FEEDER Participants**, each sorted
alphabetically by first name. Withdrawn people show separately, below, with a yellow background.

For each person you can see:

- **Name and email** — click the email to open your mail program.
- **Cell phone**, and a secondary phone if they gave one.
- **Skill level**: Novice, Beginner, Intermediate or Expert.
- **CBC experience**, as they described it when registering.
- **Equipment**: a binoculars icon and/or scope icon if they have them, "None" if neither.
- **Notes** they left for organizers, if any.
- A **Leader** badge if that row is a co-leader of your area, and an **Interested** badge if that person
  said they'd be interested in leading in future.

**Withdrawn participants** are people who registered and later dropped out. Their row is kept (in
yellow) so your headcount stays accurate, but their email is hidden — it shows "Contact via admin"
instead, since they're no longer part of your active team. Ask your organizer if you need to reach one of
them.

## Exporting your team as a CSV file

Click **Export Team CSV** to download a file containing all the contact information for your team. You can open this file in any spreadsheet program.

- It includes everyone currently shown in the **Regular** and **FEEDER** tables — **not** withdrawn
  participants.
- The file is named `cbc_area_<yourcode>_participants_<year>_<date>.csv`.
- It downloads whichever year's tab you're currently viewing, so switch tabs first if you want a past
  year's roster.

**Please treat this file as private.** It contains people's personal contact details. Don't forward it
publicly, and delete old copies once you no longer need them. If you send a group email, add the
email addresses to the BCC field to keep them private.

## Counters from past years

If your circle has run for more than one year, tabs across the top of your dashboard let you switch
between this year and up to **three previous years**. Past-year tabs are shown in orange with an archive
icon, and a banner reminds you when you're looking at historical data.

Past years are **read-only** — there's nothing to edit, only to look at and export. They're handy for
finding people who took part before and inviting them back.

## Privacy

You're trusted with your team's contact details so you can coordinate the count. A few expectations that
come with that:

- Use the information for count coordination only — planning, logistics, thanking people for taking
  part.
- Don't forward or publish the roster; use BCC if you email your whole team at once.
- Delete exported CSV files once you no longer need them.
- Don't reuse the list for anything unrelated to the count.

## Troubleshooting

**I never got my login email.** Check spam first. Make sure you used the exact email address your
organizer registered you under. Wait a minute and try again. If it still doesn't arrive, ask your
organizer to check with the server administrator.

**My login link says it's expired or already used.** Links last 15 minutes and work once. Request a new
one and click it immediately. If your email system seems to be using up the link before you do, tell
your organizer. You may need to register with a different email address. For example, birdscanada.org
uses a Trend Micro malware checker that uses up the login link before the user ever sees the email. We
haven't found a workaround yet, so Birds Canada users must register with a different email address.

**"You are not assigned as an area leader."** You're signed in with an email that isn't currently marked
as a leader for this circle. Confirm with your organizer that you're set up correctly, and that you used
the right email address.

**My team roster looks empty or wrong.** Make sure you're on the current year's tab, not a past one.
Registration may simply not have produced any sign-ups for your area yet. If it still looks wrong, tell
your organizer.

**I'm not getting the team update or weekly summary emails.** Check spam. Confirm your email address is
correct with your organizer — they can see and fix it on the Manage Leaders page. If nobody is getting
these emails at all, that's a server-side setup issue for your organizer to raise.

**The Export button doesn't do anything.** Make sure JavaScript is enabled and try a different browser.
If your team roster is empty, there's nothing to export.

**Something looks wrong or broken.** Tell your organizer what you were doing and what you saw — a
screenshot helps.
